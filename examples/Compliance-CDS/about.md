The [CubeSat Design Specification](https://www.cubesat.org/cubesatinfo)
is Cal Poly's standard for the mechanical, electrical and operational
requirements a CubeSat must satisfy to fly in a standard dispenser. It is
public domain, and Rev 14.1 supersedes both Rev 13 and the 6U CDS.

It is here as an example of the case that motivates `pages:` — an
external document a project has to comply with, where the artifact wants
to show two relevant pages rather than make the reader hunt through
thirty-four.

The file itself lives once, in `examples/datasheets/`, and is referenced
from here and from Harness-001 with different page selections. Neither
folder carries a copy.
