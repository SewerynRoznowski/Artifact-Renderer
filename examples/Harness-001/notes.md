## Assembly

1. Strip 4 mm, crimp Molex KK 254 female terminals, verify each crimp
   with a pull test before insertion.
2. Bond the shield drain to the controller-end backshell **only**. A
   shield bonded at both ends turns the harness into a ground loop.
3. Dress the bundle so the CAN pair keeps its twist to within 20 mm of
   the connector body.

## Inspection

| Check | Method | Accept |
|---|---|---|
| Continuity, each pin | 4-wire, 1 kHz | < 50 mΩ |
| Insulation, pin to shield | 500 V DC, 60 s | > 100 MΩ |
| Retention, each terminal | axial pull | > 30 N |

> Rework limit: two re-crimps per terminal position. A third failure
> means the connector body is replaced, not the terminal.
