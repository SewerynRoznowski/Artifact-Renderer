# Molex KK 254 (2.54 mm) — extract

Shared by every artifact that terminates in this connector. One copy
lives here; each artifact references it rather than carrying its own.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Pitch | 2.54 mm |
| Current rating | 4 A per circuit |
| Wire range | 0.32 – 0.81 mm² (22 – 18 AWG) |
| Mating cycles | 25 |
| Operating temperature | −40 °C to +105 °C |

Terminals are crimped, not soldered. Soldering a KK terminal wicks solder
into the crimp barrel and makes the joint brittle at the transition.
