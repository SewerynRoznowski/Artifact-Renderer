## Sequence

1. Offer the bracket up to the deck and check the keep-out envelope
   before any fastener is started — the v2 upright is 8 mm taller than
   the v1 it supersedes, and clears the harness by 4 mm, not 12 mm.
2. Seat all four base fasteners hand-tight, then torque diagonally.
3. Torque the M4 pair last, and re-check the base afterwards.

Torque values are in the schedule above; they assume dry threads.

## Handling

| Concern | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Anodising | Handle with gloves; fingerprints etch the surface over time |
| Bonding path | Do not paint or anodise the two bonding pads on the underside |
| Storage | Flat, on its base. Stored on the upright, the flange takes a set |

> The v1 bracket is not interchangeable. It shares the hole pattern but
> not the upright height, so it will bolt down and then foul the harness.
