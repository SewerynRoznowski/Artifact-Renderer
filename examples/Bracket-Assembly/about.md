A mechanical artifact, showing the three ways a part gets described:
a **drawing** for dimensions and keep-outs, a **3D model** so a reviewer
can see which part is being discussed, and a **specification** for the
numbers that have to be met.

The 3D view is deliberately just a reference view. It is there to answer
"which part is this?", not to replace CAD — the detail lives in the
drawing, the torque schedule and the notes below it.
